# Butterfly (Crocodile) Mixer

![Flaps down](../assets/how-to-butterfly-mix-flaps-down.png)

Butterfly (a.k.a. crocodile) braking raises the ailerons slightly while
lowering the flaps a lot, adding drag to control descent rate on landing
approach — with elevator compensation to counter the pitch change it
introduces.
